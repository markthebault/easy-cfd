Choose Animation > Vortex particles in the existing 2D workspace. The current
enabled scene boundaries are unioned, normalised by reference length and passed
to the browser-only WASM solver. Signed vortex domains are red (positive) and
blue (negative). Dot size/opacity are display choices. The Drag & lift panel uses
vvflow body forces, excluding image bodies. The ground at y=0 uses reflected
boundaries and
opposite-sign image vortices, regenerated after shedding and merging. It is an
impermeable slip boundary with no road boundary layer. Pressure overlays are
not included.

The core is vendored unchanged. Generated TSpace omits HDF5 save/load; generated
TMatrix adapts CLAPACK return types. Generated MEpsilonFast exposes a distance-
based merging multiplier. The browser adapter replaces desktop orchestration
and output with an in-memory API. OpenMP directives are ignored.

Experimental 2D flow only; no validated accuracy or full-vehicle force claim.
